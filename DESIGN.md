# Docera visual system

The user's pinned direction is minimal, elegant, precise professional software. Operate mode: opening and reading a document takes precedence over expressive presentation. No competitor composition is copied.

## Direction

An individual document workspace with a dark pine navigation rail, warm pale workspace, white document surfaces and compact humanist type. A numbered document-page glyph is the sole brand mark; normal web navigation, buttons, tables and controls remain familiar.

Seven grounded references considered: book colophon, correspondence stationery, archive finding aids, typographic standards manuals, conservation studio labeling, municipal library wayfinding, architectural publication indexes. Conservation labeling lends careful hierarchy, a pine identification rail and a thin double-page brand mark. This is the fifth candidate selected by the design helper; the user brief overrides decorative material treatments. Competing ticket/transit directions add discipline in status clarity and navigation, but their visual motifs would distract from document reading and are declined. No fake paper texture, metrics, testimonials or usage history.

## Tokens

DM Sans Variable, self-hosted with its OFL notice. Body 14–16px, dashboard heading 36–44px, viewer controls 13px, tabular numerals for file sizes and page counts. Heading tracking no tighter than -0.035em.

Pine `#163f3b` rail, deeper `#10302d`, ink `#1c302e`, secondary `#586965`, warm workspace `#f6f7f3`, white paper, pale green selected surfaces, forest `#23665a` actions. Amber planned badges and rust error states have readable text. Lines are thin; radii 6–10px for controls and 12px for upload. No card shadows except a subtle offset paper shadow in the viewer.

Spacing uses 4/8/12/16/24/32/48px. Dashboard: navigation rail + max-width workspace; upload is primary; recent files are a real list; tool roadmap is a categorized directory. Viewer: compact top bar, search/navigation panel, continuous page canvas; properties/editor controls are absent in M1.

## Interaction

Visible keyboard focus, reduced-motion support, real empty/loading/error states, drag feedback and network-byte upload progress. An uploaded document enters the recent list with a short background transition; no sweeping dashboard animation. Destructive removal requires explicit confirmation. On mobile the rail becomes a compact header and document navigation becomes a toggleable panel.
